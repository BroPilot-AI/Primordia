- The five playable species, tribal huts, hearths, totems and space structures in `assets/models/generated/` were generated and hand-fitted for this game. The species are rigged and animated with [Adobe Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com).
- Sprites, backgrounds, ground, water and planet textures, and shaders were made for this game.
